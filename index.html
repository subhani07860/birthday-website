first, the truth

I am sorry.

For the words I should have softened, for the silences I should have broken, and for the times I chose my pride over your heart.

I will not hide behind excuses. I hurt you, and I have carried that with me every day since.

what you are to me

You were never small in my life.

Some people pass through us like weather. You stayed like a season: your laughter, your honesty, the way you made ordinary days feel lit from within.

Seven years taught me what I only half understood then: the people who are good for our soul are rare, and I did not protect that well enough.
